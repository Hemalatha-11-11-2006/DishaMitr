Subject Combination Guide

Confused about which subjects to choose? This guide will help you understand different streams and make an informed decision about your future.

Making the Right Choice

Choosing your subject combination is one of the most important decisions you'll make as a student. It can shape your future career path and higher education opportunities.

This guide explains the four main subject combinations available to most students: Bio Math, Computer Math, Pure Science, and Commerce. Each combination has its own unique subjects, career paths, and advantages.

Bio Math

This combination combines biology with mathematics, perfect for students interested in medical and engineering fields.

Core Subjects

  • Biology
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry

Career Paths

Medical Doctor, Biomedical Engineer, Pharmacist, Biotechnology, Genetics Research

Computer Math

Focuses on computer science with strong mathematical foundation, ideal for tech enthusiasts.

Core Subjects

  • Computer Science
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Information Technology

Career Paths

Software Developer, Data Scientist, AI Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst, Game Developer

Pure Science

Focuses on fundamental sciences without mathematics, for students interested in research and science fields.

Core Subjects

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Geology/Environmental Science

Career Paths

Research Scientist, Chemist, Physicist, Geologist, Science Writer

Commerce

Focuses on business, economics, and finance, perfect for students interested in the business world.

Core Subjects

  • Accountancy
  • Business Studies
  • Economics
  • Mathematics/Statistics

Career Paths

Chartered Accountant, Financial Analyst, Business Manager, Economist, Entrepreneur
