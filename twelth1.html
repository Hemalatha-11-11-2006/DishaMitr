Planning Your College Journey

As a Class 12 student, it's crucial to start preparing for college admissions early. This guide will help you understand the process, required documents, and counseling options for different career paths.

Remember to keep all your documents organized and start preparing well in advance of application deadlines.

Engineering

For students interested in pursuing engineering degrees like B.Tech, BE, or related fields.

Entrance Exams

  • JEE Main & Advanced
  • State-level engineering exams
  • BITSAT
  • VITEEE

Required Documents

  • Class 10 & 12 mark sheets
  • Birth certificate
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • ID proof (Aadhar, passport, etc.)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Entrance exam scorecard

College Counseling Process

Understanding the counseling process is crucial for successful college admissions. Here's what you need to know:

Counseling Types

Centralized Counseling: For national-level exams like JEE, NEET

State Counseling: For state-level entrance exams

University Counseling: For individual universities

Counseling Steps

1. Registration and choice filling

2. Document verification

3. Seat allotment rounds

4. Acceptance and fee payment

5. Reporting to allotted college

Counseling Tips

• Research colleges thoroughly before choice filling

• Keep multiple options open

• Understand the fee structure and reservation policies

• Be prepared with all documents in advance
