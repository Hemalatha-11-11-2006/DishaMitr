How to create an account — easy steps for students

Follow these simple steps. If something is unclear, ask a teacher or parent for help.

What is an account?

An account is your personal area on this website where your work, progress and settings are saved. When you log in, the site knows it is you.

Think of it like a personal school diary: your email is the name on the diary and your password is the lock.

Why do we need your email and password?

  • Email: We send a short code or link to this address to make sure the account belongs to you.
  • Password: Keeps your account private — only you should know it.

Step-by-step: Create your account

1
Open the Sign Up page
Go to the Sign Up page (where you found this link). You will see boxes for Email, Password, Confirm password, and Code.
2
Type your email
Use an email you can open (for example: yourname@gmail.com). We will send a code there to check it is really yours.
3
Choose a password
Make a password you can remember but others cannot guess. Try at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
4
Confirm the password
Type the same password again in "Confirm password". If the two are different, the site will ask you to retype them.
5
Send code to your email
Click the Send code button. Check your email inbox for a short code (check Spam/Junk if you don't see it).
6
Enter the code
Type the numbers you received into the "Code" box, then click Sign up.
7
Read Terms & Privacy
These explain how we keep your information safe. Ask a parent or teacher to help you if you are unsure.
8
Finish signing up
After filling all boxes and entering the correct code, press Sign up. Your account will be created.

Quick safety tips

Do: use a strong password and log out on shared computers.
Don't: share your password with friends.

If something goes wrong

  • Can't find the code? Wait a few minutes and check Spam/Junk folders.
  • Passwords don't match? Re-type both fields carefully.
  • Forgot password later? Use the "Forgot password" link on the Login page to reset it.
  • Under 18? Ask a parent or teacher before creating an account.

Need more help? Ask your teacher, parent, or click the "Need Help" button on the Sign Up page.
