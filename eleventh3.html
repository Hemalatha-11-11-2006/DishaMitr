Understanding Competitive Exams

Competitive exams are special tests that help you get into prestigious colleges and universities. Here's what you need to know about the most common ones:

NEET Exam

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test

NEET is for students who want to become doctors and study MBBS, BDS, or other medical courses.

Important: For NEET, your 12th grade marks don't matter as long as you pass. Your NEET score is what counts for admission to medical colleges.

Subjects tested: Physics, Chemistry, Biology

JEE Exam

Joint Entrance Examination

JEE is for students who want to become engineers and study at prestigious engineering colleges like IITs and NITs.

Important: For JEE, you need to have good knowledge of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Subjects tested: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
