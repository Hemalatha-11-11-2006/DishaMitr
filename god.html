Quiz Instructions

Welcome to the Career Assessment Quiz! This quiz will help you discover your strengths, interests, and ideal career paths.

Before you begin:

  • Find a quiet place where you can focus without distractions
  • Set aside approximately 15-20 minutes to complete the quiz
  • Answer honestly based on your preferences, not what others expect
  • There are no right or wrong answers - this is about discovering your unique strengths

How it works:

  • You'll answer a series of questions about your interests and preferences
  • Based on your responses, we'll recommend suitable career paths
  • You'll receive a personalized report at the end of the quiz
