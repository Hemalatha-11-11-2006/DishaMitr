Fun Activities to Build Your Skills

Here are some enjoyable ways to develop skills in each subject area:

For Bio-Math

Start a nature journal - draw plants and animals you see, count petals on flowers, measure growth of plants.

For Pure Science

Do kitchen experiments - baking (chemistry), make a lemon battery, build a simple pulley system.

For Computer Math

Try block-based coding on Scratch or Code.org. Play logic games like Sudoku or chess.

For Commerce

Play business games like Monopoly. Set up a lemonade stand or small business with friends.

General Skill Building

Read books on topics that interest you. Watch educational shows. Ask questions about how things work.

Super Skill: Problem-Solving

Solve puzzles, play strategy games, build with LEGOs, and try to fix broken things (with permission!).

Remember These Important Tips!

  • Follow your curiosity - learn about what interests you most
  • It's okay to make mistakes - that's how we learn best
  • Ask questions - lots of them!
  • Try different activities - you might discover new interests
  • Have fun - learning is an adventure!
